Magic City Runner Privacy

Magic City Runner pairs your browser with Magic City and performs user-approved browser missions in your signed-in Chrome profile. It pauses for login, payment challenges, captcha, or policy mismatches.

What The Extension Stores

The extension stores your Magic City URL, device id, device-scoped runner token, and pairing status in Chrome extension storage on this browser.

What The Extension Sends

For a mission you start, the extension sends pairing/device status, mission-scoped browser checkpoints, approved page URLs and titles, and compact proof metadata to Magic City. It may read the visible page structure needed to search, open a product, add it to cart, and open checkout. It does not send raw payment-sheet contents.

What It Does Not Collect

The extension does not collect passwords, raw card numbers, CVV, MFA codes, wallet private keys, seed phrases, payment-sheet contents, or arbitrary browsing history. It only accesses a site after you enable it for a pending Magic City mission.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only to pair this browser, execute the mission you approved, and create mission-bound execution receipts. Magic City does not sell extension data or use it for advertising.

Control

You can remove the runner from Magic City Settings, revoke the runner token, or uninstall the extension from Chrome at any time.
