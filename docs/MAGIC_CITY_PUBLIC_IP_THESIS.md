# Magic City Public IP Thesis

Prepared: July 2, 2026
Status: Public-safe positioning draft. Do not treat this as legal advice.

## One-Line Thesis

Magic City is a proof-bearing internet-agent commerce layer for retail users.

## Public Positioning

Magic City turns ordinary natural-language requests into scoped internet work that can be planned, routed, executed, paid for, verified, and delivered by agents.

The platform is designed for a world where users do not want a corporate chatbot that merely talks. They want work completed on the internet: research, forms, checkout, files, bookings, follow-ups, and artifacts. Magic City provides the retail front end, execution container, policy layer, payment surface, and receipt trail for that work.

## What Magic City Does

Magic City coordinates four surfaces that are usually disconnected:

1. Conversation
2. Agent execution
3. Payment or credits
4. Proof, receipts, and reputation

The product separates these surfaces so users can chat naturally while execution happens in a controlled workflow container. That separation is central to the product. Chat is for intent and context. Execution is for agent selection, inputs, approvals, payment, status, artifacts, and receipts.

## Public Architecture

The public-safe architecture can be described as:

1. A user submits a request in the Magic City interface.
2. Magic City classifies the task into a workflow lane.
3. Magic City recommends a relevant agent or set of agents.
4. The user may run a Magic City built-in agent, hire a SantaClawz marketplace agent, or add their own agent.
5. Magic City creates a scoped execution package.
6. The execution widget collects only the inputs the selected agent needs.
7. Credits or wallet payment authorize the work.
8. The agent executes or coordinates the task.
9. Magic City returns artifacts, status, and a receipt trail.
10. Proof, settlement, and reputation metadata can be carried through SantaClawz and Zeko infrastructure.

## Product Differentiators

- Retail-friendly agent hiring instead of developer-only automation.
- Agent selection inside the chat flow, without forcing users into a marketplace first.
- Execution widgets that keep high-stakes task inputs separate from casual chat.
- Support for free built-in agents, paid marketplace agents, and user-owned agents.
- Credit-backed and stablecoin-native payment paths.
- Agent receipts and proof metadata as first-class product objects.
- Privacy-aware local context and connected-account policy boundaries.
- Zeko-backed proof and authorization as an automation layer, not a user-facing burden.

## Ecosystem Role

Magic City is the retail front end. SantaClawz is the agent protocol and marketplace rail. Zeko is the proof, authorization, privacy, and automation substrate. Base USDC/x402 is the agent-native settlement path.

This split lets Magic City feel simple to users while preserving protocol-native execution beneath the surface.

## Public Claim Language

Preferred public phrase:

"Magic City is a proof-bearing internet-agent commerce layer that lets users hire agents to complete real work online, with credits, wallet payments, execution receipts, and protocol-backed reputation built in."

Stronger phrase for investor or partner conversations:

"Magic City is a retail front end for SantaClawz/Zeko-powered agent execution: chat for intent, widgets for execution, credits or stablecoins for payment, and receipts for proof."

Avoid claiming "first ever" publicly until a novelty search and patent review are complete.

Safer claim:

"Magic City is among the first known retail agent-hiring interfaces combining conversational routing, execution widgets, credit/stablecoin payment orchestration, marketplace agents, and proof-bearing receipts."

## What Not To Publish Yet

Do not publicly disclose the following without counsel and product review:

- detailed patent claim charts;
- private invention disclosures;
- unpublished payment-risk controls;
- sponsor-wallet or treasury-control internals;
- agent-ranking heuristics beyond high-level factors;
- anti-abuse logic;
- confidential partner or agent data;
- screenshots or traces from unreleased execution flows;
- private Zeko proof schemas that may be patent-relevant.

## Relationship To Existing Categories

Magic City is not merely a chatbot, browser automation tool, payment widget, or agent directory. The distinctive product category is the combination:

Natural-language request -> scoped internet-agent execution -> payment/credits -> artifacts -> receipts/proof -> reputation.

That loop is the Magic City wedge.
